# Client Brief: Kenyon Men's Golf

## Who runs the site

Three people would post. The head coach owns the tournament schedule, the travel lineup, and anything aimed at recruits. He's fine with email and spreadsheets but doesn't want to learn new software in the middle of the season. A team captain posts the weekly practice schedule. The coach enters qualifying results. A team manager uploads photos and short tournament recaps after events. None of them are technical, and all of them would post from a phone at least half the time.

## Who reads it

Players check it most. They want to know when practice is, where qualifying is, and who made the travel lineup. Parents want to know where the next tournament is and how to follow live scoring. Recruits and their families want to see results, the schedule, and how to contact the coach. Alumni want to know how the team is doing.

## What's broken now

Almost everything goes through the team group chat and coach emails. Important details get buried under other messages within a day, so the same questions come up every week: when is practice, where is qualifying, am I traveling. The official athletics site has results, but someone outside the team updates it, sometimes days later, and it has nothing about practice or travel. Recruits get the same basic information over and over in individual emails.

## What would make them say "this is better"

A player can open one page on their phone and see this week's schedule and the lineup without scrolling through a group chat. The coach updates the lineup once instead of texting it to eight people. A recruit can find the team's results and the coach's contact info without having to email first.

## What must not happen

- Only the coach can change the travel lineup or qualifying results. Players can't edit their own scores or standings.
- Only the coach can edit the recruiting page, since anything aimed at recruits has to follow NCAA rules.
- Only the coach can delete past results. Editors can never delete them.
